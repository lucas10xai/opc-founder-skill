# Vietnam notes: marketing

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Channels Vietnamese buyers use
- Facebook pages, personal profiles and Facebook groups (community selling is normal).
- Zalo (personal chat, groups) and Zalo Official Account (Zalo OA) for broadcast and customer service.
- TikTok (short video, live selling) and TikTok Shop.
- Marketplaces: Shopee, Lazada. Many buyers compare there before buying anywhere else.
- Google Business Profile and Google Maps: reviews and photos decide many local shop and food visits.
- Pick two or three channels for your buyer, not all of them. Use the panel and `/founder-consumer` to choose.

## Creators
- KOC (everyday reviewers, micro creators) are cheaper and often more trusted than KOL (big names).
  Start with a handful of KOCs and a trade of product for an honest video before paying a large fee.
- Ask for past results (views, comments, orders), not just follower count. Never buy fake reviews.

## Ads, realistically
- Facebook and TikTok ads can work with small daily budgets; test three creatives, one audience, one week.
- Record cost per message or per order, not clicks. Cost levels move, so read your own account, not a blog.
- Reviews, photos and a fast reply on Zalo or Messenger often beat extra ad spend.

## Tone and hooks
- Choose the address terms (xưng hô) on purpose: friendly (mình, bạn, em) suits young buyers and food;
  more formal (quý khách) suits services and older buyers. Stay consistent across channels.
- Lead with the concrete benefit, a real price, and proof (photos, reviews, a local address). Avoid copy
  translated from English. Test with a native speaker.

## Calendar hooks
- Tết, 8/3 (Women's Day), 20/10 (Vietnamese Women's Day), 11/11 and 12/12 (sale days), 20/11 (Teachers' Day).
  Plan content and stock 3 to 4 weeks before each date you will use. Check platform campaign calendars.

## Claims and rules
- Advertising claims (health, "number one", comparisons, before and after, promotions) are regulated.
  Check the current rules with the authority before you publish; do not rely on memory. Start at
  https://vbpl.vn (legal database) and https://moit.gov.vn. Sellers on e-commerce sites should also
  check the registration notice process at https://online.gov.vn.
- Run `/founder-compliance` for the checklist and keep proof for every claim you make.
