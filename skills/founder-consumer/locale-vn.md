# Vietnam notes: consumer panel

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Profile settings
Set these in the panel profile for Vietnamese buyers:
- `"currency": "VND"`, `"income_period": "month"`, `"language": "Vietnamese"`.
- Income in VND per month, whole numbers (for example 12000000 for 12 triệu).
- Optional `"names"`: a list of Vietnamese full names matching the segment and region (north, central, south).
  Use real-sounding common names; do not use names of real public figures.

## Segments to consider
- Students (sinh viên): small budgets, high price sensitivity, heavy TikTok and Facebook use.
- Office workers (nhân viên văn phòng): time poor, delivery apps, convenience and quality matter.
- Households (hộ gia đình): family budget, buy for others, trust and value for money.
- People working away from home (người đi làm xa): send money home, shop online, miss home food.
- Small business owners (dân kinh doanh nhỏ): price and margin conscious, buy in bulk, ask for invoices and credit.
- Pick the segments from your own research, not from this list alone.

## Habits worth modelling
- Order with Grab or ShopeeFood; ask in Zalo groups and Facebook groups before buying; watch TikTok live sales;
  read reviews and look for photos from real buyers; compare on Shopee.
- Cash on delivery (COD) is still normal for many online orders.

## Common objections
- Price (giá): "is it cheaper on Shopee or from the stall".
- Trust in the seller (tin tưởng người bán): new shop, no reviews.
- Shipping (ship): fee, speed, damage.
- Authenticity (hàng thật hay giả): real or fake goods.
- Comparison with Shopee and TikTok Shop listings.
Add the objections that fit your product so the panel can raise them.

## Honest limits
- Simulated buyers are not customers. Treat the output as a projection to test, then confirm with real
  Vietnamese buyers (`/founder-interview` or a pre-order test).
