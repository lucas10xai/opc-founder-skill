# Vietnam notes: offer design

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Offer mechanics that are common in Vietnam
- Combo (bundles at a set price), freeship over a threshold, tặng kèm (free gift with purchase).
- Loyalty card (thẻ tích điểm), stamps, vouchers, first-order discount, referral perks.
- Deposit (đặt cọc) for custom or high-value orders; pre-order lists via Zalo or a Facebook form.
- Instalments (trả góp) for larger tickets: offered through banks or finance partners, so check the cost and
  who bears it before promising.
- Zalo group perks: early access, group-only price, quick support. Keep the group moderated.
- Price anchors against Shopee and delivery apps: make the value of your offer obvious next to them.

## COD (thanh toán khi nhận hàng)
- Buyers expect to pay on delivery for many online orders. Risks: refusals, fake orders, return shipping cost.
  Add a rule: deposit for large or custom orders, confirm by phone or Zalo, and track the refusal rate as a cost.

## Guarantees (đổi trả, hoàn tiền)
- Say plainly what you accept: time window, condition, who pays shipping, how refunds are paid.
- Consumer protection law sets obligations on sellers. Confirm what applies to your product and your sales
  channel with the authority or a professional before you publish a guarantee. Start at https://vbpl.vn
  and see `founder/compliance.md` from `/founder-compliance`.
- Marketplaces have their own return rules; read them before relying on them.

## Check
- Test the offer wording with five Vietnamese buyers, and run it through the panel with `"language": "Vietnamese"`.
- Do not discount below your unit economics. Re-check the margin in VND whenever you add freeship or a gift.
