# Vietnam notes: competitors

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Where to look
- Google Maps: search the category and the area; note rating, number of reviews, hours, photos, price level.
- Facebook pages and Facebook groups: posts, prices, comments, how fast they reply, how many follow.
- Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop listings: price, sales count, shop rating, review text, shipping fee.
- Grab and ShopeeFood menus: delivery prices usually include platform markup, so compare with in-shop prices.
- Zalo OA and websites: price lists, combos, promotions.
- Review comments (Google, Facebook, marketplaces): repeated complaints are the gap you can serve.

## How to cite
- For each competitor row record: name, the exact page link, the date you looked, and the price you saw.
  Write "price seen on <date> at <link>". Never state a price you cannot link.
- Take a screenshot of price pages that change often, and save the file name in your notes.
- Quote reviews exactly in Vietnamese with the link; add an English translation if needed, labelled as yours.

## Vietnam specifics
- Informal competitors (home sellers, stalls, groups) often matter more than registered brands. Include them.
- Many sellers run both a shop and a marketplace store at different prices. Note both.
- Prices vary by district; record the area. Promotions cluster around Tết, 8/3, 20/10, 11/11 and 12/12, so
  note whether the price you saw was a campaign price.
- Do not invent competitors or guess their prices. If a number is not visible, write "not published".
