# Vietnam notes: launch

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Opening culture
- A soft opening (khai trương thử) before the public opening is common. Friends, family and nearby
  neighbours test the flow and write the first reviews. Ask them for honest feedback and Google reviews.
- Opening day customs: lì xì (lucky money) offers, flowers or gifts from friends and partners
  (hoa chúc mừng), opening-day discounts, sometimes a short ritual. Follow your own and your team's
  preferences; do not impose beliefs on anyone.
- Many owners pick an auspicious date (ngày tốt) through a calendar or an elder. This is optional. If you use it,
  set a window of two or three dates so permits and stock lead times still fit.

## Timing around Tết
- Launching in the weeks before Tết can ride gift and shopping demand, but suppliers, couriers and staff are
  stretched and permits can slow. Launching after Tết avoids the rush and the cash squeeze, but demand
  restarts slowly. Decide with the CFO's cash view and `/founder-compliance` lead times.
- Fix leave dates for staff early. Tell customers your Tết opening hours.

## Pre-order and waitlist
- Run the real-world test with a Zalo group or a Facebook form: price, date, deposit if any, and a clear
  success line. Count paid pre-orders, not likes.
- Record real buyers' answers; compare them with the simulated panel and trust the real buyers.

## Countdown additions
- Business registration, tax code and e-invoice setup (see `founder/compliance.md`).
- Food safety, fire safety and signage approvals where they apply.
- Google Business Profile verification (can take days, so start early), Zalo OA, TikTok and Facebook pages.
- Check marketplace seller onboarding time if you will sell on Shopee, TikTok Shop or Lazada.
