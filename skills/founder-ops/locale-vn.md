# Vietnam notes: operations (Vận hành)

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Business form (hình thức kinh doanh)
- Common starting forms: household business (hộ kinh doanh), private enterprise (doanh nghiệp tư nhân),
  limited liability company (công ty TNHH). Ask an accountant (kế toán) which fits: liability, tax method,
  invoicing duty, ability to sign with bigger customers or platforms, and ease of adding partners.
- Registration is done through the business registration portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn
- Household business tax rules changed from 2026 (the old lump-sum method was being replaced). Check the
  current rule at https://gdt.gov.vn and with your accountant. Do not copy a rate or threshold from a blog.

## Tax, invoices, insurance (confirm each; put the result in the cost list)
- Tax code (mã số thuế): who gets one and when. Lookup: https://tracuunnt.gdt.gov.vn
- E-invoices (hóa đơn điện tử): whether and from what revenue you must issue them, and which provider.
  Portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn
- Staff social insurance (BHXH) and health insurance: a payroll cost to confirm per employee type.
  Info: https://baohiemxahoi.gov.vn
- Keep a monthly accountant fee line in the budget even if small.

## Permits and premises
- Food or drink: food safety certificate or registration (an toàn thực phẩm) may apply. Authority:
  https://vfa.gov.vn and your ward or district office. Check which one applies to your menu and scale.
- Fire safety (PCCC) for premises: depends on size and use. Ask the landlord what the building already has.
- Signage: shop signs and street frontage often need local approval. Ask the ward office before ordering a sign.
- Run `/founder-compliance` for the full sourced checklist.

## Lease and fit-out
- Typical practice: a deposit (đặt cọc) of a few months and rent paid several months ahead. Negotiate the
  number, get it in writing, and confirm who owns fit-out and what happens if you leave early.
- Check the landlord can legally lease the space for business use (ask for the ownership paper).

## Suppliers and wholesale
- Look at wholesale markets and distributors in your city (for example chợ đầu mối for produce), local
  specialist suppliers, and platforms like Shopee and Lazada for packaging and small equipment.
- Ask three suppliers for a written quote with minimum order, delivery fee and payment terms.

## Payments, delivery, software
- Customer payment: cash, bank transfer, VietQR, MoMo, ZaloPay, VNPay. Put each fee on your list.
- Delivery: Grab, ShopeeFood, Be, GHN, GHTK, Viettel Post. Each has a commission or fee and COD rules.
- Shop software: KiotViet, Sapo, Haravan, MISA. Compare monthly fee, e-invoice support and offline mode.

## Cash handling
- One person owns the till, count at open and close, bank cash daily, and keep transfer screenshots
  matched to orders. Separate personal and business money from day one.

## Tet (Tết)
- Demand and supply swing around Tết: suppliers close, staff travel home, wages and bonuses (thưởng Tết)
  are expected. Plan the leave rota and a cash buffer three months ahead. Decide opening days in advance.
