# Vietnam notes: brand and naming

Vietnam notes for this skill. Prompts for you to check, not rules. Rules change (several tax and invoicing
rules changed in 2025 and 2026), so confirm anything specific with the authority or a professional.

## Name checks for Vietnamese
- Say the name aloud and test the meaning in Vietnamese with tones. Then drop the diacritics (dấu) and read it
  again: many names turn into something rude or silly. Test both ways with three native speakers.
- Check short names for regional pronunciation (north, central, south) and for how easy they are to spell
  in a Zalo or Facebook search without accents.
- Avoid names that sound like another known brand in the same category.

## Trademark (nhãn hiệu)
- Search the national IP office before you print anything: https://ipvietnam.gov.vn (use its trademark search
  and filing service from there; the page layout changes, so follow the official links).
- Check the name in your product class and similar classes. A professional (IP attorney) can confirm
  similarity risk. Filing takes time, so start before launch and note the lead time in `founder/launch.md`.
- Do not claim the registered symbol until it is actually registered.

## Domain
- `.vn` signals a local business and helps local search trust; `.com` is easier to say and to expand with.
  Ideally own both. Register `.vn` through an accredited registrar listed by https://www.vnnic.vn
  and confirm current registration requirements there.

## Social handles
- Reserve the same handle on Facebook, TikTok, Zalo OA, Instagram and YouTube if free, and a Google
  Business Profile with the same name and address. Use one spelling without accents across all of them.
- Write the logo and sign text with correct diacritics, and also supply a no-diacritic version for URLs.

## Practical
- Vietnamese fonts: pick fonts that display full diacritics. Test the logo with a long accented word.
- Packaging text: Vietnamese labels are often required for goods sold locally. Check requirements for your
  product category with the authority, via `/founder-compliance`.
