Use these official places. Rules for businesses, tax and invoicing were changing in 2025 and 2026, so verify
every rule on the live site before relying on it. Nothing here is legal advice; confirm with the authority or
a professional.

## How to use
- Start at the public service portal to find the procedure, the office, the documents, the fee and the
  processing time. Then confirm at the local office (ward, district or provincial) because practice varies.
- Search the legal database for the exact document and read its effective date. Documents get amended; check
  whether a newer one replaced it.
- Many sector permits sit with a provincial department. Find yours through the public service portal.
- Local specifics (fire safety, signage, premises) are handled by local authorities. Ask the ward or district
  office and write down the name and date of the answer.

## What changed recently (verify)
- Household business taxation and invoicing rules were being reworked from 2026.
- E-invoice rules were updated by new decrees in 2025 and 2026.
- Some procedures moved online or changed offices. Always check the live page, not a blog post.
